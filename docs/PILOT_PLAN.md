# 60 Day Pilot Plan

## Scope
One department (suggested: advising or IT support), 20 to 50 staff.

## Success goals (shown live on the Management page)
| Goal | Target |
|---|---|
| Questions answered without a help desk ticket | 70% or more |
| Staff who finish the safety training | 80% or more |
| Helpful rating (thumbs up share) | 80% or more |
| Sensitive data leaks | 0 |

## Value estimate
The Management page estimates staff hours saved (minutes per answer, default 6) and office visits avoided (default 30% of answers). The office can adjust both in Admin settings.

## Timeline
1. Week 0: office reviews its answers in Knowledge base owners; security reviews settings.
2. Weeks 1 to 8: staff use it daily; the weekly knowledge gaps digest goes to each office.
3. Week 8: results report (Copy report), staff quotes (Pilot quotes), decision on expansion.
