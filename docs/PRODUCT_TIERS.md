# Product Tiers (draft)

Pricing is a starting point for discussion, per staff member per month, billed yearly.

| Tier | For | Includes | Suggested price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assist | Any department | Role based assistants, answers from official sources, guided tour, training modules, tickets | $5 |
| Assist + Overseer | Offices handling sensitive data | Everything in Assist, plus the overseer, case queue, Management dashboard, audit log, alerts | $10 |
| Enterprise | Whole university | Everything above, plus single sign on, help desk and SIEM integration, custom assistants per department, admin settings, quarterly security report | Custom |

**Pilot package:** one department for 60 days, setup and training included, success goals agreed in advance (see `PILOT_PLAN.md`).

**Add ons:** custom assistant build, knowledge base setup per office, student facing version, accessibility audit.
